# TV Test video clips

Short test clips for the video test in the TV Test app for Android TV and Google TV.
The app downloads each clip, plays it once and deletes it again.

The clips are test patterns in 1080p and 4K, at 24 to 120 frames per second, in H.264, HEVC,
VP9 and AV1, in SDR, HDR10 and HLG.

More TV tests: https://tv-test.com
